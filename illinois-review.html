How the Illinois Shines value changes

Illinois Shines REC prices vary by project location/group and AC system-size band. Group A generally includes Ameren Illinois, MidAmerican, Mt. Carmel, and MISO-area municipal/co-op utilities. Group B generally includes ComEd and PJM-area municipal/co-op utilities.

These are published 2026–27 REC price references, not a quote of total incentive value. Total REC contract value depends on the approved project, expected REC delivery, contract terms, verification, eligibility, and other program requirements. Customer ownership, tax treatment, financing and project structure can materially change the economics.

Conditional customer-owned Small DG adder: Illinois Shines states that qualifying customer-owned Small DG projects may receive a $20/REC adder in the 2026–27 Program Year only if all program conditions are met, including that the project does not receive the federal ITC or residential clean-energy tax credit. This page does not assume eligibility for that adder.
