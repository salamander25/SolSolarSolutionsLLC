Start with what matters

A review built around your need.

Business owners, facility managers, property owners and authorized representatives evaluating one or more commercial sites.

Property & authority

Clarify who controls the roof, parking or land, how long the business expects to use it and who can authorize a project.

Load & operating pattern

Business hours, interval usage and demand patterns can change a project’s value. Public opening hours are not a substitute for energy data.

Buildability & timing

Roof life, parking use, access, utility processes and business continuity need attention before a design or installation schedule is promised.

A useful commercial review connects operations and economics.

Start with the facility type and the areas you want to consider. A warehouse roof, a busy parking lot and an open parcel each involve different constraints. The initial inquiry helps organize the right conversation with the property, operations and financial stakeholders.

A later technical and commercial review can compare likely production, utility treatment, demand patterns, equipment and proposed contract terms. Sol coordinates qualification and business development; licensed design, installation, financing and other delivery responsibilities are project-specific and must be established separately.

For a portfolio, begin with one representative site and note the number of additional locations. A successful review at one property does not automatically establish feasibility at another. Site-specific evidence keeps the expansion decision useful.

Before you begin.

Can I explore solar for a leased facility?

Yes. Tell us your role and what is known about property permission. The owner’s authority and relevant lease terms must be addressed.

Can you assess more than one site?

Indicate the number of sites and start with a representative location. Each location needs its own coverage and feasibility review.

Are incentives included automatically?

No. Current program rules and project eligibility require separate verification. No incentive or tax result is guaranteed.
