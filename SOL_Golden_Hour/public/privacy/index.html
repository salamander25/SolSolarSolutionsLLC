Preview only · September 12, 2026

Your information in this preview.

The public design preview prepares your brief in the browser. It does not send your name, email, phone, project answers or consent choices to Sol, a CRM or an email service. Closing or reloading the page discards the in-memory form. A saved brief stays wherever you choose to save it.

Isolated test service

When this source package runs with its isolated test server, the form clearly labels that mode before submission. Synthetic test inquiries are stored in a separate SQLite database and held for review. This service is not connected to customer campaigns, email, SMS, scheduling or a live CRM. Use synthetic information only. The test database has no public read endpoint.

Technical requests

The hosting provider necessarily receives requests to serve this preview. No advertising pixels or external analytics are installed. Attribution is limited to allowlisted campaign codes; personal details are never put into analytics events. The production processors, retention period and rights-request contact have not yet been approved.

Media choices

The solar stock image is served with this preview. Manufacturer video is not fetched until you select “Load GT3 overview.” That action contacts PUDU’s media host, which may receive your IP address and browser request information. Official product links open the publisher’s site.

Contact choices

Request-specific permission and optional marketing email are separate, unchecked choices. Marketing permission is never required for a request. Email-only means no calls. This preview does not authorize marketing calls or texts, does not enroll you in a campaign and does not overwrite an existing opt-out.

Before production

A production policy must identify the actual inbox, processors, sharing, retention, deletion and applicable privacy choices before collection is enabled. This preview notice is not the approved production privacy policy.
