Good questions.
Clear answers.

Where can Sol help?

Share your location first. Service coverage, supplier availability and the appropriate delivery team must be confirmed for your project.

Can I explore more than one solution?

Yes. Choose the main need first and add related interests later. Each solution is evaluated on its own requirements.

Can robotics start virtually?

Yes. Begin with a conversation, remote site information and official product material. Sol has no GT demo unit.

What will it cost and how long will it take?

Pricing and timing depend on the site, scope, supplier and required reviews. This initial inquiry does not create a price, finance offer or installation commitment.

How will you use my information?

The preview prepares a brief locally unless a clearly labeled isolated test service is connected. Production contact and data-processing arrangements are awaiting approval. See the preview privacy notice.
