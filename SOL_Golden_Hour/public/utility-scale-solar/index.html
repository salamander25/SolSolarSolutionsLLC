Start with what matters

A review built around your need.

Landowners, authorized brokers, developers and project stakeholders with a nonconfidential utility-scale opportunity.

Land & role

Explain the location, approximate acreage and your relationship to the opportunity. Distinguish ownership, option, representation and early exploration.

Power & grid evidence

Grid proximity is a clue. Documented utility correspondence, studies and the applicable interconnection process carry different evidentiary weight.

Stage & next decision

An idea, a controlled site and a project with studies have different needs. Share the stage and what you want a next conversation to resolve.

An origination conversation that respects project reality.

Early infrastructure decisions benefit from a clear account of what is known and what is still an assumption. Location, land area, access, development stage and utility territory help frame a first conversation. They do not establish buildability, capacity, permits or commercial viability.

Sol’s role is origination, qualification, business development and project coordination. Licensed engineering, EPC work, installation, utility approvals, financing and other delivery functions require appropriate counterparties and project-specific agreements. An inquiry is not an offer of capital or a development commitment.

Keep the initial description nonconfidential. Do not upload title files, confidential contracts, site security details or utility documents here. If deeper due diligence is appropriate, document-sharing terms and a secure channel should be agreed first.

Before you begin.

Does a nearby substation mean capacity is available?

No. Physical proximity does not establish interconnection feasibility or available capacity. Utility evidence and the applicable studies are required.

Can I submit an opportunity before controlling the land?

Yes. State your actual role and stage accurately. Site control remains a separate qualification question.

Is Sol the engineering or EPC contractor?

Sol is the origination, qualification, business-development and coordination contact. Delivery scope and counterparties are established for each project.
