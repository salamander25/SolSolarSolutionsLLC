# Reference research and design decision

Reviewed September 12, 2026. These are editorial fit judgments for Sol, not a popularity or conversion leaderboard. First-party pages were opened and read. Desktop browser frames of all five lead references were inspected, alongside page text. Apple was rechecked after its first incomplete frame; some initial Verkada and Equinix media/font rendering was incomplete, so their specific copy observations rely on the corroborating text extraction. Mobile behavior and completed reference forms were not tested. No third-party traffic, heat-map or conversion estimates are claimed.

## Five lead references

| Fit rank | Reference | First understanding and actions | Grouping, evidence and friction | Principle adapted for Sol |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Tesla Energy](https://www.tesla.com/energy) | Energy for home and larger applications; quote/advisor actions and product exploration | Home storage is separated from commercial/utility paths. Large product/architecture media carries the story. No inline lead form was observed in the extracted energy page; downstream form not tested. Desktop hero visually reviewed; mobile untested. | Route distinct energy buyers early. Keep one understandable action and a quieter exploration route. Use original art rather than Tesla photography or layout. |
| 2 | [Stripe](https://stripe.com/) | A broad financial-infrastructure promise with self-service and sales routes | Products sit under coherent business needs, followed by product demonstrations and attributable customer evidence. Entry actions defer most form friction. Desktop reviewed; localized text redirected to en-pl. Mobile and signup not tested. | One company promise can lead to multiple useful paths. Sol’s evidence must remain its own; no borrowed metrics or visual signature. |
| 3 | [Equinix](https://www.equinix.com/) | Infrastructure for distributed AI, with solution exploration and expert contact | Products and stakeholder roles are both navigable; infrastructure examples and customer material support the offer. Contact is a separate route, not a tested form. Text and initial desktop visual review; mobile untested. | Ask about site, requirement, evidence and decision stage. Sol must describe its coordination role without adopting an operator’s capabilities. |
| 4 | [Apple at Work](https://www.apple.com/business/) | Devices and services for businesses, organized through readable topic sections | Product examples, linked evidence and business stories lead toward a business contact form. The extracted form requests contact, company and role information plus a separate update choice. Form not submitted. Desktop rechecked after initial loading; mobile untested. | Use clear editorial hierarchy and purposeful whitespace. Keep personal contact preferences explicit; do not copy the scale of Apple’s initial qualification form. |
| 5 | [Verkada](https://www.verkada.com/) | A unified physical-security offer; demo action plus optional overview | Product and use-case navigation, customer stories and platform proof surround the action. Homepage extracted without an inline contact form; downstream demo flow untested. Text and initial desktop visual review; mobile untested. | Pair a visible next action with optional product understanding. For Sol, offer a virtual fit conversation, never an unavailable physical demo. |

Golden Hour is recommended because it can serve both approachable home/business conversations and serious infrastructure qualification. Daybreak is a plausible alternative for a predominantly residential audience. Command is a plausible alternative for an infrastructure-focused campaign. These are hypotheses, not measured preferences.

## Technical references and limitations

[Google’s Core Web Vitals documentation](https://web.dev/articles/vitals) specifies good thresholds of LCP ≤2.5 seconds, INP ≤200 milliseconds and CLS ≤0.1, assessed at the 75th percentile. These are release targets. No field dataset or Lighthouse score was obtained for Sol.

[WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) is the review target: normal text 4.5:1, large text 3:1 and essential nontext controls 3:1. The 48px controls are a design choice. Sampled contrast, keyboard and responsive checks are not a conformance certification; a full assistive-technology and real-device review remains open.

No Sol traffic or sales cohort data was available for a measured baseline. The brief’s historical Unbounce statistic is not used as a Sol forecast. Establish Sol’s own qualified-opportunity and collected-business baseline after a verified launch.
