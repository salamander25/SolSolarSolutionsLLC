## Six substantive static pages

| Route | Audience and problem | Offer / evidence | Qualification and next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/residential-solar/` | Homeowner / authorized representative assessing home energy | Practical solar/storage fit; explicit roof, tariff and backup questions | General location, authority, solar vs solar+storage, optional usage, timing; check coverage first |
| `/commercial-solar/` | Owners and facility teams assessing property and operational fit | Site, load pattern, buildability and portfolio considerations | Facility type, authority, roof/carport/ground, optional usage and site count, timing |
| `/battery-storage/` | Home, commercial and infrastructure storage buyers | Purpose and complete-system requirements; power distinguished from energy | Purpose, existing solar, optional critical loads/duration, kW/MW and kWh/MWh separately |
| `/robotics/` | Facilities, grounds, hospitality and logistics teams | Task-first virtual review; official GT3 and CC1 Pro references | Task category, workflow, optional area and surfaces; supplier-supported review, no Sol GT demo |
| `/utility-scale-solar/` | Landowners, authorized representatives and developers | Evidence-led origination; no capacity inference from proximity | Site role, approximate acres/MW, utility, stage, utility-evidence status, timing |
| `/data-center-infrastructure/` | Developers, landowners and infrastructure stakeholders | Site, power and delivery evidence; no cloud-hosting offer | Site role, initial/ultimate MW, desired delivery date, stage, utility-evidence status |

Each page has its own H1, title, meta description, audience, three considerations, substantive explanatory copy, next step, FAQs and optional related paths. These are reusable static page implementations, subject to content and commercial approval before production. They are not JavaScript heading swaps or a complete ongoing SEO program.

Robotics follow-up varies by selected task: grounds care asks about turf, slope, obstacles, access, operating windows, charging and storage; cleaning asks surfaces, usable area, obstacles and current workflow; delivery/transport asks routes, transitions, payload and human interaction; supervised inspection requires a permitted task and human operating plan. These follow-ups are deliberately not a long required first form.

## Copy boundaries

Infrastructure scope is origination, qualification, business development and project coordination. Delivery scope and counterparties are project-specific. No engineering, EPC, installation, utility, capital-provider, manufacturing or operating credential is implied. Do not publicly name restricted counterparties.

Sol has no GT demo unit. Do not offer an in-person Sol GT demonstration or physical pilot, imply inventory, invent GT variants or publish unverified prices, warranty, service coverage or commissioning terms. Southern California is the intended sales motion; supplier territory is not yet verified, so no territorial promise appears.

No guaranteed savings, incentives, backup, power capacity, delivery date, rankings or profitability. Images identify stock or manufacturer material. Public copy contains no internal commissions, CRM IDs or private notes.
